## Gradients (not used)

No gradients were added. The user chose to keep the brand system flat and let photography, the brush artwork, and the established color blocks carry visual energy.

## Tone and voice

### Words to use

Write with warmth, patience, and conviction. Address people directly with “you” and “we.” Prefer words such as pray, gather, belong, grow, serve, hope, together, and worldwide. Invitations should feel open and encouraging.

### Words to avoid

Avoid manufactured urgency, corporate language, and hype. Do not use “leverage,” “synergy,” “revolutionary,” “act now,” or “limited time.” Avoid excessive exclamation marks and vague spiritual superlatives.

### Voice example

> Wherever you are, you can join the prayer watch. Come as you are, pray with others, and grow into a steady life of intercession.

## Usage dos and don'ts

**Do:**

- Use semantic tokens such as `bg-background`, `text-foreground`, and `bg-primary` for general interface styling.
- Use the named PHANET utilities for intentional brand moments: `bg-midnight-green`, `bg-crayola`, `text-mikado-yellow`, and `text-cerulean`.
- Use the accessible semantic primary when placing small white text on a Crayola-colored button.
- Keep spacing on the Tailwind 4 px grid.
- Use Fraunces selectively for high-impact editorial display headings.
- Use `font-mono tabular-nums` for counters, times, and references.
- Test both light and dark modes.

**Don't:**

- Introduce unrelated accent colors when an existing PHANET color can do the job.
- Use the original Crayola swatch behind small white text without checking contrast.
- Apply Fraunces to body copy, navigation, forms, or administration screens.
- Use `transition-all`; name the animated property.
- Add gradients without a deliberate future brand decision.
- Override semantic tokens per component when the system token should be corrected instead.
